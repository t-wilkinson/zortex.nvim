Attributes

Attributes are @key(value), or a bare @word, anywhere on a line as long as they start a word (me@example.com is text). Most have a short form: @a(4.1) for @area(4.1), @sm for @size(sm). In Neovim, typing @ completes them and :Zortex attributes shows this list.
